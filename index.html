About

Made by Abdullah Sajid

Engine Simulation simulates each cylinder's gas dynamics (intake runners, valves, combustion, exhaust primaries and collectors) about 10,000 times per second and turns the exhaust pressure into sound in real time. It is a JavaScript port of Engine Simulator by AngeTheGreat. The engine presets use the values from that project's example engine scripts, and the exhaust impulse responses come from its sound library.

Keys: I ignition, S starter (hold), Q W E R throttle steps, Space full throttle while held, ↑ ↓ shift gears, N neutral, M automatic gearbox on or off, Shift clutch (hold), B brake, D dyno hold. Drag the 3D view to orbit it and scroll or pinch to zoom.
